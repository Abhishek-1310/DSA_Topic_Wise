Inorder Traversal
Given a root of a Binary Tree, your task is to return its Inorder Traversal.

Note: An inorder traversal first visits the left child (including its entire subtree), then visits the node, and finally visits 
the right child (including its entire subtree).

left root right
Examples:
        1
      /   \
     2     3
    / \
   4   5
Input: root = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]    
Output: [4, 2, 5, 1, 3]
Explanation: The inorder traversal of the given binary tree is [4, 2, 5, 1, 3].
