Important BST Interview Facts
✅ Left subtree values are smaller than the node.
✅ Right subtree values are greater than the node.
✅ Inorder traversal of a BST is always sorted.
✅ Search, insert, and delete all take O(h) time, where h is the tree height.
✅ Duplicate keys are usually not allowed (or are handled consistently by placing them all on one side, depending on the implementation).
✅ Minimum = leftmost node.
✅ Maximum = rightmost node.
✅ Inorder successor = smallest node in the right subtree.
✅ Inorder predecessor = largest node in the left subtree.
✅ A balanced BST has height O(log n), while a skewed BST has height O(n).
