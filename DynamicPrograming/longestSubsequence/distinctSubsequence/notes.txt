Distinct Subsequences
Count how many different ways t can be formed as a subsequence of s.
Important: We are counting ways, not finding the longest subsequence.
Example
s = "babgbag"
t = "bag"
There are 5 different ways to form "bag" from "babgbag".
You can choose different positions for:
b → a → g
Even though the resulting string is the same "bag", different index selections count as different ways.

3️⃣ Why prev + dp[j] instead of 1 + prev?
This is the biggest difference from LCS.
In Distinct Subsequences, we're counting ways.
When:
s[i-1] == t[j-1]
we have two choices:

                 match
                  ↓
             s[i-1] == t[j-1]
                /       \
             TAKE       SKIP
              ↓           ↓
          prev         dp[j]

So we add both possibilities:
dp[j] = prev + dp[j];
